01 / Research

Designing and understanding
Human–AI collaboration.

My work connects Information Systems, Human–Computer Interaction, and Natural Language Processing. I investigate how the design of AI-enabled systems shapes human behavior, task outcomes, and organizational processes, with learning and knowledge work as key application settings.

01

Designing Human–AI
collaborations

How should people and AI work together? I design and evaluate intelligent assistants and conversational agents, connecting interaction design with human task outcomes and learning.

Explore research systems ↗
02

Adaptive systems
and processes

How can support fit the person, task, and moment? I combine computational modeling, behavioral data, and process mining to personalize feedback and understand work and learning processes.

Process mining for learning ↗
03

Human agency
and responsible AI

When should people rely on AI, and when should they question it? I study trust, reliance, bias, and value-sensitive design to understand the human consequences of intelligent systems.

Research on bias in AI feedback ↗

Research approach. I combine design science, computational modeling, experiments, and qualitative and quantitative user research. My aim is to contribute both actionable design knowledge and explanations of how intelligent systems affect people in context.

04 / Teaching & mentoring

Teaching how to understand
and shape digital systems.

I teach Information Systems through the connection between people, data, processes, and technology. Students analyze organizational problems, build prototypes, and evaluate the opportunities and consequences of AI.

My courses combine conceptual foundations with practical labs, prototyping, and reflection. Teaching spans process management and mining, design and data science, and generative AI. In mentoring bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral researchers, I emphasize independent inquiry, methodological rigor, and constructive feedback.

05 / Academic profile

An interdisciplinary perspective
on people, AI, and organizations.

I lead an interdisciplinary research group at BFH and collaborate across Information Systems, HCI, and AI. My work connects the design of interactive systems with questions about human behavior, capabilities, and their use in organizational contexts.

Research perspective

From individual interactions
to systems and processes.

I approach Human–AI collaboration as a socio-technical question: how do the capabilities of AI, the design of an interface, and the context of use shape what people can achieve?

Writing, reasoning, and reflection offer rich settings for studying these questions. Through this work, I examine broader issues of feedback, trust, agency, and skill development in digitally mediated work and learning.
